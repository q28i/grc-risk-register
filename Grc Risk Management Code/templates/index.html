Qualitative Risk Model Reference

NIST SP 800-30

Deterministic scoring standard evaluated across Likelihood (1-3) and Impact (1-3):

Score = Likelihood × Impact
  • Low Risk (1-2): Minimal operational threat; periodic review.
  • Medium Risk (3-4): Moderate concern; scheduled remediation required.
  • High Risk (6-9): Critical threat; immediate treatment plan needed.
